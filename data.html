DATA

Our Data Resources

We bring together longitudinal clinical records, molecular testing, imaging, and laboratory data to support cancer research and AI development. Our current data resources include the ROOT HEALTH NSCLC cohort at Samsung Medical Center.

Cohort definition: 38,154 patients with NSCLC, who received anticancer treatment (Systemic therapy, surgery, radio therapy and clinical trials etc.) at Samsung Medical Center. Data cutoff: September 18, 2026.
