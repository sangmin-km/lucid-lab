ROOT HEALTH / Clinical Variables

CLINICAL VARIABLES

ROOT HEALTH includes comprehensive longitudinal clinical data covering patient characteristics, diagnosis, treatment, molecular testing, follow-up, and clinical outcomes.

Clinical Variables Available in ROOT HEALTH

38 summarized variable groups across 7 clinical domains.

Clinical Domain Summarized Subcategory Data Description Data Included / Coverage Data Value (Examples)
Registry / Common Cancer registry identifiers Registry source and identifiers used to define each cancer case in ROOT HEALTH. Includes whether the case is linked to cancer registration data, cancer registration year/month, registration serial number, and cohort application date. Registration year refers to the year recorded through Korea's national cancer registration framework (Korea Central Cancer Registry, KCCR). Source: registered / non-registered; Year: YYYY; Month: MM; Serial no.; Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Basic Information Patient demographics Core demographic characteristics. Date of birth, sex, nationality status, and residential area. Date; M/F; Korean/foreign national; province/city/district
Basic Information Clinical trial participation Participation in clinical trials. Participation status and number of clinical trial enrollments. Y/N; count
Basic Information Cohort classification Predefined clinical/molecular cohort flags used for research-ready patient selection. EGFR-sensitive, ALK-positive, IO, rare-mutation, CNS metastasis and cohort-exclusion classifications. Cohort flags / predefined categories
Basic Information Visit / follow-up Key hospital visit and follow-up dates. Last follow-up, first oncology visit/admission, physician ID and last visit type. YYYY-MM-DD; visit type; physician ID
Basic Information Body measurements Integrated anthropometric measurements. Height, weight, BMI, measurement date and source. cm; kg; BMI; YYYY-MM-DD; source
Follow-up & Outcomes Vital status / death Vital status and mortality information from integrated clinical/registry sources. Death status/date, source of death date, cause-of-death code and cause descriptions. Y/N; YYYY-MM-DD; ICD/code; text/source
Follow-up & Outcomes Recurrence Post-treatment recurrence information. Recurrence status/date/source, including local and distant relapse. Initially/Recurred; Y/N; YYYY-MM-DD; source

Follow-up & Outcomes Survival outcomes Derived survival endpoints. Overall survival (OS), disease-free survival (DFS) and progression-free survival (PFS), including event/date/time variables. Event Y/N; date; survival time

Medical History & Status Family history Cancer-related family history. Family history status and related information. Y/N / categorical
Medical History & Status Comorbidities Pre-existing medical conditions relevant to clinical characterization. Multiple comorbidity indicators derived from clinical information. Y/N / condition-specific indicators
Medical History & Status ECOG performance status Functional performance status. ECOG performance status and related timing information. ECOG 0–4; date
Diagnosis Diagnosis dates Key dates defining cancer diagnosis. Initial diagnosis and institution-specific diagnosis dates. YYYY-MM-DD
Diagnosis Primary diagnosis Primary cancer diagnosis information. Primary diagnosis name/code. Diagnosis code / text

Diagnosis Clinical TNM stage Clinical TNM staging. Clinical T, N, M and overall stage across applicable AJCC editions. cT / cN / cM / Stage

Diagnosis Multiple primary cancer Information on synchronous/metachronous additional primary cancers. Presence and characteristics of additional primary malignancies. Y/N; site/diagnosis/date
Examination EGFR EGFR molecular testing summarized at the gene level rather than by repeated result fields. Initial and repeat EGFR mutation results, detailed mutation information, EMR-derived results and cell-free testing. Initial/repeat; tissue and cell-free molecular results. Positive/negative/no mutation; mutation notation; date
Examination ALK ALK testing summarized across available modalities. ALK IHC, FISH and EMR-derived ALK results. IHC; FISH; EMR-derived result. Positive/negative; score/result; date
Examination PD-L1 PD-L1 expression testing summarized across assay types. PD-L1 results from major IHC assays. 22C3; SP263; SP142; other assay. TPS/percentage or categorical result; date
Examination NGS Next-generation sequencing information. NGS test availability and related test/result information. NGS. Panel/test information; date/result
Examination ROS1 ROS1 testing summarized across available modalities. ROS1 PCR and IHC results. PCR; IHC. Detected/not detected or positive/negative; date
Examination BRAF BRAF molecular testing. BRAF mutation test result and related mutation information. Molecular mutation testing. Positive/negative; mutation notation; date
Examination Bronchoscopy Bronchoscopic examination information. Bronchoscopy procedure, timing and related examination information. Bronchoscopy. Procedure/result/date
Treatment Overall treatment sequence Longitudinal treatment history summarized as sequential treatment episodes. Treatment type, intent, timing and related treatment information across sequential treatment records. Up to 7 sequential treatment records plus last-treatment summary. Treatment type; date; intent; sequence
Treatment Surgery Lung cancer surgical treatment history. Surgical information including procedure-related variables and repeated operations. Up to 3 surgery episodes. Procedure; date; surgical details
Treatment Salvage treatment Salvage/local treatment after prior therapy. Salvage treatment type and related information. Salvage treatment. Treatment type / date

Treatment Definitive therapy Definitive non-surgical treatment including systemic/chemoradiation-related treatment records. Regimen and treatment-course information for definitive therapy. Up to 3 definitive treatment lines/courses. Regimen; start/end date; treatment-related fields
Treatment Durvalumab Consolidation durvalumab treatment information. Durvalumab treatment timing and related variables. Durvalumab. Treatment dates / treatment status

Registry note. Korea's national cancer registration program is coordinated through the Korea Central Cancer Registry (KCCR), a nationwide cancer registration program operated under the National Cancer Center. In this summary, “cancer registration year” refers to the year recorded for the cancer case in that registration framework.
