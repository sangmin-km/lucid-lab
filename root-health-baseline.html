Footnotes

  • Data are presented as n (%). EGFR exon 19 deletion and L858R percentages are shown among tested and mutation-positive patients; 10 patients with both mutations are included in both groups. ALK low expression was classified as negative. NA indicates an unavailable or uninterpretable test result. PD-L1 (22C3) is reported as TPS.
  • TNM reference implementation years: seventh edition, 2010; eighth edition, 2017 internationally (AJCC, 2018); AJCC Version 9, 2025. Institutional adoption dates were not confirmed.
  • Clinical and pathological stage percentages were calculated using the total cohort (N=38,399), including patients with missing stage data.
  • Pathologic stage includes patients who underwent surgery after neo-adjuvant therapy.
  • First-line EGFR/ALK TKI regimens are included in the first-line palliative treatment category. “Others” includes patients treated at SMC who did not meet the definitions of curative-intent surgery, definitive CCRT, or first-line palliative treatment.

Abbreviations

AJCC, American Joint Committee on Cancer; ALK, anaplastic lymphoma kinase; CCRT, concurrent chemo-radiotherapy; EGFR, epidermal growth factor receptor; NGS, next-generation sequencing; NSCLC, non-small cell lung cancer; PCR, polymerase chain reaction; PD-L1, programmed death-ligand 1; TKI, tyrosine kinase inhibitor; TNM, tumor–node–metastasis; TPS, tumor proportion score; WT, wild type.
