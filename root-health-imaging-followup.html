Data/Imaging, Follow-up & Laboratory Data

IMAGING, FOLLOW-UP & LABORATORY DATA

ROOT HEALTH includes large-scale imaging, outpatient follow-up, and laboratory data collected across the longitudinal NSCLC care pathway. The sections below retain the counts and metrics summarized in the current ROOT HEALTH workbook.

Cohort definition: 38,154 patients with NSCLC, excluding stage 0, who received cancer-directed treatment at Samsung Medical Center. Data cutoff: September 18, 2026.
