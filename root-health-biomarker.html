GENOMIC & BIOMARKER DATA

ROOT HEALTH includes molecular and biomarker testing data for major actionable and clinically relevant biomarkers in NSCLC. The table below preserves the testing modalities, patient coverage, and total test volumes summarized in the current ROOT HEALTH workbook.

Cohort definition: 38,154 patients with NSCLC, excluding stage 0, who received cancer-directed treatment at Samsung Medical Center. Data cutoff: September 18, 2026.
