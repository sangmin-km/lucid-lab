ROOT HEALTH

What is ROOT HEALTH?

ROOT HEALTH is a research-ready oncology data platform that integrates longitudinal real-world clinical information for cancer research. It brings together registry information, patient characteristics, follow-up and outcomes, diagnosis and staging, molecular testing, treatment history, imaging, outpatient follow-up, and laboratory data.

The current NSCLC cohort includes patients who received cancer-directed treatment at Samsung Medical Center and is designed to support longitudinal, multimodal clinical research and AI development.

Cohort definition: 38,154 patients with NSCLC, excluding stage 0, who received cancer-directed treatment at Samsung Medical Center. Data cutoff: September 18, 2026.

ROOT HEALTH Platform

ROOT HEALTH organizes routinely generated real-world data into a research-ready infrastructure for disease-specific cohort generation, longitudinal analysis, and multimodal AI research.

Data Available in ROOT HEALTH

Explore the major data components summarized in the current ROOT HEALTH clinical-variable workbook. Each section will be presented on a separate page with the original variables and counts organized into readable tables.

Clinical Variables

Registry/common information, demographics, clinical trial participation, cohort classification, visits and follow-up, body measurements, vital status, recurrence, metastasis, survival outcomes, medical history, ECOG performance status, diagnosis, TNM staging, molecular examinations, and treatment history.

View clinical variables

Genomic & Biomarker Data

Testing coverage and test volumes for EGFR, ALK, PD-L1, BRAF V600E, RAS, ROS1, NTRK, HER2, MET exon 14 skipping, HER2 IHC, MET IHC/FISH, and overall NGS testing.

View biomarker data

Annual Biomarker Testing

Diagnosis-year-specific testing trends from 2008 through 2026, including the number and percentage of patients tested for EGFR, ALK, PD-L1, and NGS.

View annual testing data

Imaging, Follow-up & Laboratory Data

Imaging volumes for Chest CT, Brain MRI, and PET; outpatient follow-up metrics; and 25 laboratory test types including blood counts, chemistry, coagulation, inflammatory markers, CEA, and renal-function measurements.

View imaging, follow-up & lab data

Data cutoff: September 18, 2026.
